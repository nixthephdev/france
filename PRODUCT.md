## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A personal website for Olga France Blas-Obias. The user confirmed that the primary visitor action is to read her insights. The site also introduces her background, interests, speaking, projects, advocacy, and opportunities to collaborate.

## Users

Readers interested in France's perspectives on entrepreneurship, business strategy, sustainability, mentorship, and thought leadership. More specific audience demographics and reader research remain open decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing implementation: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and customised shadcn/ui components. Sanity publishing, enquiry email, and production hosting remain unconfigured.

The only supplied photographs are a close-up portrait and an ASEAN event photograph. The event photograph does not establish a speaking role. No approved biography, articles, testimonials, client logos, project outcomes, or speaking video have been supplied. Illustrative editorial content must be labelled as such.

LinkedIn is the current working contact destination. Preserve keyboard navigation, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and meaningful image descriptions.

## Brand Commitments

The client's stated direction: minimalist, sophisticated, warm, and purpose-driven; professional without feeling overly corporate. Her supplied profile line is “Growing businesses, people and purpose”.

The user rejected the first design as ugly and generic despite the installed skills. Its cream, olive, terracotta, repeated italic headings, identical section treatment, and decorative shapes are rejected implementation choices, not a required visual identity. A redesign is authorised.

## Open Decisions

Preferred public display name, final biography and article copy, publication cadence, project evidence, and the production domain remain unconfirmed.
