# Design direction and content handoff

## Audience and intended journey

Working audience hypotheses: potential collaborators, event organisers, readers interested in business and sustainability, and people seeking mentorship. Validate these priorities with France.

The homepage moves from identity and a clear invitation, through her story and areas of focus, into perspectives, speaking, project evidence, and a final contact invitation. The order is a design hypothesis, not a proven conversion result.

## Decisions

- Keep warm ivory, olive, terracotta, editorial headings, and generous spacing.
- Use the supplied portrait as the visual introduction, keeping her face clear across responsive crops. The supplied ASEAN event photo adds context beside Speaking & Mentorship, with a neutral attendance caption.
- Use plain navigation labels and a consistent contact action.
- Place real evidence alongside claims once available. Never fill credibility gaps with invented logos, metrics, testimonials, or affiliations.
- Show sample articles as editorial concepts. Preview dialogs explicitly distinguish them from France's published work.
- Use shadcn/ui for keyboard-aware mobile navigation and dialogs, styled to the brand. Components complement Next.js and Tailwind.
- Keep readable line lengths, visible focus states, descriptive headings, and reduced-motion support.
- Contact currently opens guidance and a real LinkedIn link. It does not imply that an email or enquiry has been submitted.

## Content to request from France

1. Preferred public name, a 60-word introduction, and a longer approved biography.
2. A portrait with permission to use it; ideally a high-resolution image with room around the subject.
3. Two or three speaking or work photographs; an optional captioned speaking clip and video poster.
4. Two or three projects: challenge, her exact role, partners allowed to be named, dates, and substantiated outcomes.
5. Two initial insights or existing articles with permission to republish.
6. Confirmed speaking themes, audience, format, mentorship scope, and preferred enquiry contact.
7. Approved testimonials and client or organisation logos only if she wants them and has permission.
8. Primary website goal: speaking enquiries, partnerships, mentorship, or growing readership.

The portrait and ASEAN event photograph are supplied assets. Project imagery remains a visibly labelled placeholder; a speaking video has not been supplied. Biography and proposed topics are drafts. CMS, email delivery, and analytics require separate implementation and configuration.

## Validation

Check mobile navigation, dialog focus containment, Escape dismissal, focus return, anchor targets, horizontal overflow, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, readability, and production compilation. Before launch, test the site's key tasks with representative visitors; visual appeal does not establish usability by itself.
